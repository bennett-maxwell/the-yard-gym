Franchise Opportunity Profile

The Yard Gym

Boutique strength and conditioning studios from Australia: pre-recorded 50-minute RIG, TURF, and PAY DAY group classes in compact 2,000-4,400 sq ft facilities. Every fact on this page is sourced from the TYG Enterprises, LLC Franchise Disclosure Document (issued December 8, 2025), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$50,000

Conversion franchisees: $25,000. Progressive 5% multi-unit discount (up to 20%). Largely non-refundable. (FDD Item 5, pp.7-9)

Estimated Initial Investment

$398,550 to $954,100

Start-up franchisee. Conversion range: $236,550 to $539,100. (FDD cover; Item 7, p.22)

Royalty

7% of Gross Revenue

Greater of 7% or $1,600/month, collected as $400/week. Conversion gyms exempt 8 weeks. (FDD Item 6, p.12)

Marketing Fee

2% of Gross Revenue

Greater of 2% or $200/month into the Marketing Fund. Plus 3% of gross revenue/quarter required local marketing. (FDD Item 6, pp.12-13, 18-19)

Territory

Protected, non-exclusive

Never smaller than one ZIP code. Franchisor reserves nontraditional venues and chain acquisitions. (FDD Item 12, p.50)

Term

5 years + two 5-year renewals

Post-term non-compete: 2 years, 5-mile radius of any Franchised Gym. (FDD Item 17, pp.59-62)

Facility

2,000 to 4,400 sq ft

Pre-recorded 50-minute RIG / TURF / PAY DAY classes; a floor coach on the floor every session. (FDD Item 1, pp.2-3)

Training

Initial Training Program — no fee

Primary Owner and management complete Training and Coach Training modules before opening. (FDD Item 11, p.38)

Equipment

$112,500-$122,500 exercise pack

Initial Exercise Pack purchased from TYG Affiliate; plus $3,000-$9,000 additional fitness equipment. (FDD Item 7, p.21)

Franchisor

TYG Enterprises, LLC (Delaware, 2023)

HQ: 1/2 Cawarra Road, Caringbah, NSW, Australia. US office: West Hollywood, CA. Founded 2020 by Dan and Tiarne Bova. (FDD Item 1, pp.1-2)

System Size

9 US gyms open · 60 in AU/NZ

55 more US franchise agreements signed but not open (as of June 30, 2025). (FDD Item 1, p.2; Item 20, pp.67-68)

Financing

Not offered

Franchisor offers no direct or indirect financing. (FDD Item 10, p.37)

Item 19 — Financial Performance

Measurement periods: Oct 1, 2023 – Sep 30, 2024 and Oct 1, 2024 – Sep 30, 2025. 2 US Franchised Gyms (both in San Diego, California) were open for the full 12-month periods and are reported.

  • Franchise 1 (opened Jan 28, 2023): $781,220 gross revenue, Oct 2024 – Sep 2025
  • Franchise 2 (opened Jul 15, 2023): $697,773 gross revenue, Oct 2024 – Sep 2025
  • Average of the two reporting gyms: $739,496
  • Confidence: LIMITED — n=2 gyms in one city, unaudited and unverified franchisee sales reports; revenue only, no expenses or profit figures

Some outlets have achieved these sales; individual results may differ; there is no assurance you will achieve these results. Item 19 reports revenue only — no payback period can be computed. (FDD Item 19, p.65.)

Risk Profile

34 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate-low. A young US system growing quickly (2 to 9 gyms in two years) with zero disclosed litigation, zero closures, and audited financials — tempered by a narrow Item 19 (n=2 in one city) and a capital-intensive build-out.

Risk inputs (documented, weighted):

  • High capital ceiling: investment tops out at $954,100 — pushes score up
  • Early-stage US system: 9 open gyms; Item 19 covers only 2 — pushes score up
  • Minimum royalty floor: $1,600/month regardless of revenue — pushes score up
  • HQ outside US (Australia) — pushes score up
  • Non-exclusive territory with franchisor reserved rights — pushes score up
  • Zero terminations / zero gyms left the system — pushes score down
  • Audited financial statements attached (Exhibit J) — pushes score down
  • No litigation disclosed — pushes score down

Method: base 15 + 10 high capital ceiling + 8 early-stage US system + 5 minimum royalty floor + 5 HQ outside US + 3 non-exclusive territory − 5 zero terminations − 5 audited financials − 2 no litigation = 34. Published so an independent verifier can recompute it.
